# AGENTS.md

## Project Purpose

This is Yan Zeng's personal academic and intellectual website. It includes academic profile material, research notes, essays, public life reflections, and approved AI-assisted writing.

## Privacy Rules

Never publish or expose:

- Visa or immigration information.
- Addresses, housing details, or location-sensitive routines.
- Family members' private information.
- Financial, tax, or employment records.
- Unpublished paper drafts unless explicitly marked public.
- Private faculty, department, colleague, or student communications.
- Raw AI chat logs.
- Medical or personal health details.
- Emotionally reactive or temporary judgments.

## Content Rules

- Only files in `content/public/` may be rendered by the site.
- Files in `content/drafts/` are unapproved drafts and must not be rendered.
- Files in `content/private/` must never be used by the website.
- Files in `content/secret-source/` are plaintext secret sources and must never be committed except for the README.
- Only encrypted payloads in `public/secret/` may be published for the `/secret/` page.
- Never put passwords, passphrases, or plaintext secret content in source code, Markdown, docs, Git history, or commit messages.
- Every public post must have frontmatter:
  - `title`
  - `date`
  - `category`
  - `status: public`
  - `tags`
- Drafts may be generated by AI, but publication requires human approval.

## Style

- Use clear academic prose.
- Avoid overclaiming.
- Prefer concise paragraphs.
- Support English, Chinese, and bilingual content.
- Preserve a calm public voice even when source notes are exploratory.

## Engineering Rules

- Keep the site static and easy to deploy.
- Prefer Markdown content over custom code for ordinary notes.
- Do not add automation that publishes content without review.
- Keep the public, draft, and private content boundaries explicit.
- Treat the secret section as encrypted sharing for low-risk trusted-reader notes, not as a place for high-risk personal records.
