Life notes can record public-facing reflections on reading, movement, place, and ordinary routines. They are useful when they connect lived experience to intellectual work without turning private life into public inventory.

The boundary matters. Public life writing should avoid addresses, administrative details, family information, finances, health records, and anything that should not become a durable public trace.

For this site, the aim is a modest one: preserve selected reflections from life in and around Irvine while keeping the private background private.
