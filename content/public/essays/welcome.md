This site begins as a small public notebook. It is not meant to collect everything, and it is not meant to automate disclosure. Its purpose is to make selected ideas easier to find, revise, and share.

The publishing rule is simple: AI may help draft, but a human must review before anything becomes public.

That rule keeps the site useful without turning ordinary thinking into automatic performance.
